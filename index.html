Good food shouldn't end up in the trash.

One day at high school, an administrative problem sent everyone home. Mr. Calderón, who runs the cafeteria, had already prepared many lunches, but he had no customers and lost all his sales.

It doesn't only happen to him. NovaMeall exists to connect both sides and help businesses recover sales they were about to lose.

How NovaMeall works

Three simple steps turn the day's unsold food into a great deal.

1

Businesses list what's left

Restaurants, bakeries and cafés publish the available items at closing time, synced in real time.

2

You find it nearby

Open the app, see what's available on the map and chat with the business if you have questions.

3

Save money and pick it up

Pay less for tasty food while the business recovers its investment instead of throwing it away.

We only win if the business wins.

Unlike traditional delivery apps that charge high commissions on the regular menu, we focus exclusively on saving the unsold item of the day. A fair and transparent model that turns losses into value.
